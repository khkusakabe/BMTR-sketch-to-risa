How to use
  1. Open a PDF page or a photo of a sketch.
  2. Set scale: click both ends of a known dimension and type its length, for example 24'-6" or 7.5 (feet).
  3. Draw members: click node to node. Clicks snap to nearby nodes. Hold Shift to lock horizontal or vertical. Right-click or Esc ends a chain.
  4. Supports: click a node to cycle its restraint.
  5. Find lines (optional): drag a box around the framing. Suggested members show dashed. Remove extras with Delete, then Accept.
  6. Export: copy the tables into RISA-3D spreadsheets, or import the DXF.

Pan: drag with the right or middle button, or hold Space. Zoom: mouse wheel.
