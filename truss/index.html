    Truss
    Key plan layout

    With more than one truss, the export repeats the truss along Z at the spacing above, so you get the layout from the key plan in one model.

    Unit loads

    Two load cases: TC UNIT on every top chord member and BC UNIT on every bottom chord member, uniform over the full length in global −Y, no self-weight. Each gets its own load combination.

    Defaults by member role

    Continuous = no release (plate-connected chord carried through the joint). Pinned = RISA BenPIN (Myy and Mzz released, torsion kept for stability).
